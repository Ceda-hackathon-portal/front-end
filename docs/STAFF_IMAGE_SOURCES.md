# Staff hero photograph sources

The stock photographs listed below were selected and visually inspected on 20 November 2026, with no people visible in those delivered crops. They illustrate the work area; they do not depict CEDA premises, infrastructure or funded projects. No AI image generation or paid stock licensing was used.

Images are hosted locally, so staff pages do not send image requests to a stock-photo provider. The stock images are resized/cropped to 1800 × 950 pixels and compressed as JPEGs; the shared hero adds the CEDA colour overlay in CSS.

The staff homepage (`/admin`) now reuses `public/images/Carousel2.jpg`, the second public-homepage slideshow photograph showing a man holding a Raspberry Pi, supplied in landing-page commit `e9dcaee`. This change was requested on 21 November 2026. The original file is reused without image editing; CSS positions the crop. The previous `staff-overview.jpg` is retained for the fallback hero.
